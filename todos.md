### 2. Exploration Jump Requires Equipment
**Design:** [planning/jump-drive-equipment.md](planning/jump-drive-equipment.md)
Jumping from the exploration screen (via M key / MAP button) requires a special "FTL Jump Drive" equipment item installed on the ship. Without it, the player can only jump at warp gates. Enabled by default in debug mode.

### 3. Ship Outfitter UI
**Design:** [planning/ship-outfitter.md](planning/ship-outfitter.md)
Create a visual ship outfitting screen accessible from the Outfitter station tab. Show the player's 3D ship model as background with equipment slots (weapons, engine, jump drive, shields, etc.) that can be viewed and changed. Intuitive drag-and-drop or click-to-equip interface.

### 4. Supply Chain Visualization on Galaxy Map
**Design:** [planning/supply-chain-map.md](planning/supply-chain-map.md)
When at a station's trading screen, show the galaxy map with supply chain overlays: what the current system consumes/produces, trade links to other nodes, shortages, surpluses, and active convoys. Clicking a node shows its own supply chains. Convoys are physical entities that can be escorted, pirated, or destroyed, with immediate economic effects on their destination.
